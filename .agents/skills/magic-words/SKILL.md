# Magic Words & Social Etiquette Skill: The Top Magic Words and When to Use Them

This skill empowers primary and kindergarten learners to master the **Top Magic Words** of polite communication, understand the empathy and social purpose behind each phrase, and recognize the exact real-world moments ("When to Use Them") across home, school, sharing, meals, and playground situations.

---

## 🎯 Core Pedagogical Philosophy

1. **Empathy-First Learning**:
   - Magic words are not just polite rules; they are **keys that unlock kindness**, make other people feel valued, and turn conflicts into friendships.
2. **Child Deduces Independently**:
   - The learner should always read or hear the real-life situation first and figure out which magic word fits best before any hint or answer is given.
3. **Strictly Hidden Answers by Default**:
   - **Never reveal the answer or explanation in open text** within challenge prompts.
   - All solutions, golden rules, and roleplay scripts must be enclosed within a **collapsed `<details><summary>...</summary></details>` state**.
   - In interactive multi-turn conversations, withhold the answer until the child has attempted a response or asked for help.
4. **Bilingual Enrichment (English + Marathi / Devanagari)**:
   - Provide Marathi equivalents for every magic word to support dual-language learners (e.g., *Please* = *कृपया*, *Thank You* = *धन्यवाद*, *Sorry* = *माफ करा*, *Excuse Me* = *ऐका ना / माफ करा*, *You're Welcome* = *काही हरकत नाही*).
5. **Tone & Body Language Coaching**:
   - Remind learners that magic words must be accompanied by a **warm smile**, gentle eye contact, and sincere voice tone.

## 🪄 The Top Magic Words Catalog

| Magic Word | Marathi Equivalent | Core Purpose / Feeling | Golden Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Please** | **कृपया (Krupaya)** | Asking with respect & kindness | Turns a bossy demand into a kind request. Always say it when you want something! |
| **Thank You** | **धन्यवाद (Dhanyavaad) / आभार** | Gratitude & appreciation | Say it whenever someone gives you a gift, helps you, or pays you a compliment. |
| **Sorry** | **माफ करा (Maaf Kara) / क्षमस्व** | Empathy, repairing mistakes | Say it when you make a mistake, hurt someone accidentally, or break something. |
| **Excuse Me** | **माफ करा / ऐका ना (Aika Na)** | Polite interruption & space | Say it to get someone's attention, pass between people, or after a sneeze/cough/burp. |
| **You're Welcome** | **काही हरकत नाही (Kaahi Harkat Nahi)** | Warm acceptance of thanks | Say it when someone thanks you. It shows you were happy to help! |
| **May I...?** | **मी ... करू का? (Mee ... Karu Ka?)** | Respectful permission | Say it before borrowing things, entering a room, or joining a group. |
| **Pardon Me** | **मला क्षमा करा / परत सांगा** | Clarification & gentle apology | Say it when you didn't hear what someone said and want them to repeat it gently. |
| **Good Morning / Night** | **शुभ सकाळ / शुभ रात्री** | Warm greeting & connection | Start and end the day with cheerful words that bring a smile to family & friends. |

## 🧭 "When to Use Them" Scenario Matrix

### 1. School & Classroom Manners 🏫

| Situation / Trigger | What Happened | Magic Word | Marathi | Example Dialogue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Borrowing Supplies** | You need an eraser or blue crayon from your desk partner. | **Please** | कृपया | *"Aarav, please may I borrow your blue crayon?"* |
| **Teacher Gives Books** | Teacher hands you your corrected notebook or homework sheet. | **Thank You** | धन्यवाद | *"Thank you, Teacher!"* |
| **Entering Late / Doorway** | The classroom door is closed and the teacher is teaching. | **May I...?** | मी ... करू का? | *"May I come in, Teacher?"* |
| **Asking for Water** | You feel thirsty during class and need to take a sip. | **May I...?** | मी ... करू का? | *"Teacher, may I drink water, please?"* |
| **Passing Between Friends**| Two classmates are talking in the hallway and blocking the walkway. | **Excuse Me** | माफ करा / ऐका ना | *"Excuse me, may I please squeeze past?"* |
| **Sneezing in Class** | You had a sneeze or sudden cough during reading time. | **Excuse Me** | माफ करा | *(Cover with elbow)* *"Excuse me!"* |
| **Did Not Hear Question** | The teacher asked you a question, but the ceiling fan was noisy. | **Pardon Me** | मला क्षमा करा / परत सांगा | *"Pardon me, Teacher, could you please repeat that?"* |
| **Dropping Someone's Pen** | You accidentally knocked your friend's pencil case off the desk. | **Sorry** | माफ करा | *"I'm sorry! Let me help you pick up your pencils."* |

## 📋 Challenge Output Templates

When generating practice questions or interactive prompts, use one of the templates below with **collapsible `<details>` blocks**.

### Template 1: Real-Life Situation Mystery Card

```markdown
### 🪄 Magic Word Challenge: What Should You Say?

🏫 **Scene:** School Classroom  
🧒 **Situation:** Rohan wants to color his picture of a sunflower, but he forgot his yellow crayon at home. He turns to his desk partner, Maya.

❓ **Question:** What magic word should Rohan say when asking Maya for the yellow crayon?  
- A) Sorry
- B) Please
- C) Excuse Me
- D) You're Welcome

<details>
<summary>💡 Peek Magic Word & Golden Rule</summary>

✨ **Correct Answer:** **B) Please (कृपया)**  
🌟 **Golden Rule:** Whenever you ask someone for an item or a favor, always say **"Please"**. It turns a demand into a polite and friendly request!  
💬 **Polite Dialogue:** *"Maya, please may I borrow your yellow crayon?"*  
</details>
```

### Template 2: Fix the Bossy Sentence (Magic Wand Upgrade)

```markdown
### 🪄 Magic Wand Upgrade: Turn Bossy into Kind!

⚡ **Bossy Sentence:** *"Give me that toy car right now!"*  
❓ **Challenge:** Wave your magic wand! Rewrite this sentence using two magic words so it sounds polite, warm, and friendly.

<details>
<summary>💡 Peek the Magic Upgrade</summary>

✨ **Upgraded Polite Sentence:**  
> *"May I please play with that toy car when you're finished?"*  
> or: *"Please may I have a turn?"*  

🌟 **Why It Works:** Adding **"May I...?"** and **"Please"** shows respect and makes your friend feel happy to share with you!
</details>
```

## 🎭 Interactive Roleplay Game: "Magic Word Detective"

When playing in interactive mode, present one short scenario at a time:
1. Describe the scenario vividly with child characters and emoji props.
2. Ask the child: *"What magic word will you whisper or say out loud?"*
3. After the child answers:
   - If correct: Celebrate with high-fives and explain *why* that magic word touched the other person's heart!
   - If incorrect or hesitant: Give a friendly hint about how the characters feel, then reveal the word with its Marathi meaning.
